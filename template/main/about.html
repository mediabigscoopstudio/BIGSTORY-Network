About BIGSTORY Network

At BIGSTORY Network, we envision a world where content is a tool for transformation. We strive to be a driving force in shaping an informed, just, and empowered society by telling the stories that matter most — stories that challenge power, amplify unheard voices, and ignite meaningful change. Through compelling, purpose-driven content, we aim to bridge divides, elevate human potential, and inspire action that leads to lasting social impact and build a more informed, empowered, and equitable society.

Our Mission

Telling stories that move minds and mobilize change...

BIGSTORY Network is built on the belief that journalism should serve the people, not power. We are committed to uncovering the stories that others overlook, digging deeper to raise the issues that truly matter.

Our mission is to spotlight the unheard voices, expose opportunity, and inspire change — through fearless expression of words and people-powered storytelling. By connecting issues to impact, we empower individuals and society to think critically, act boldly, and build a more just, equitable world.

Why BIGSTORY Network Is Needed

In a time when truth is often lost in noise, BIGSTORY Network was born out of necessity — to bring light to untold stories and voices that shape our nation from the ground up.

Impactful storytelling is not just journalism — it’s a tool for awareness, social change, and justice. When the spotlight is turned on real issues and real people, the ripple effect can transform minds, communities, and policies.

This platform stands as a bridge — between the unheard and the unconcerned. Because change begins with a story that moves us.

Meet Our Founder

Sseema Giill

Founder | CEO | Chief Editor

Sseema Giill is an inspiring media professional, Founder-CEO of Screenage Media Pvt. Ltd. and founder of the Association for Gender Equality (AGE). For a commitment to social impact, she champions women’s empowerment and gender equality.

With a heart full of stories and a vision to amplify unheard voices, Sseema Giill laid the foundation of BIGSTORY Networks. Her passion for equal empowerment and media innovation has driven the company to become a platform where authenticity meets creativity. From journalism to digital content, Sseema's journey is fueled by purpose and people. Her leadership blends empathy with excellence, making BIGSTORY Networks a trusted name in impactful content creation.
