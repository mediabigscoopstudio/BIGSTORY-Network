AGE India

Empowering Women Through Equality, Purpose & Dignity.

Our Vision

To establish an impactful and sustainable model that empowers women, fosters dignity, and brings equality in all walks of life – work, home, and society. AGE India focuses on gender equality through advocacy, education, and opportunity creation.

Our Mission

  • Advance women’s human rights and financial independence
  • Promote feminist leadership and gender equality practices
  • Engage youth in social transformation and elder care

Sseema Giill – The Force Behind AGE India

Sseema Giill is a dynamic media professional and the visionary founder of AGE India. Raised in a conservative Jaat family, she overcame personal hardships, including the early loss of her father, to support and uplift her family of six women. With a strong background in journalism and development communication, Seema transformed her struggles into a mission for social change. She champions gender equality, women’s financial independence, and inclusive leadership. Through AGE India and Big Story, she empowers women, inspires youth, and builds platforms for voices that matter. Her journey is a testament to resilience, purpose, and the belief that change begins with courage.
