Our Initiatives
Five Pillars of Green India's 360° Impact Model
📖Storytelling

Green Stories

Impact storytelling series showcasing real changemakers, sustainable innovations, and grassroots entrepreneurs — especially women-led initiatives across India.

👩‍🌾Women

Women for Green

Empowering women entrepreneurs in sustainability, agri-based solutions, handicrafts, and eco-friendly businesses from Tier 2, Tier 3, and rural India.

🗣Policy

Green Dialogues

A discussion platform bringing policymakers, corporate leaders, and innovators together around sustainability, policy, and scalable solutions.

🛠On-Ground

Green Action Labs

On-ground implementation through workshops, community campaigns, and pilot projects. Local challenges tackled with community-driven solutions.

🔗Ecosystem

Green Network

Connecting startups, innovators, investors, policymakers, and institutions working towards sustainability and inclusive growth at scale.

This Movement Is For You
An inclusive platform for everyone who believes growth and sustainability must go hand in hand
🎓

Youth & Women Entrepreneurs

Empowering the next generation of leadership. Whether you are a student or a woman building a green enterprise, we provide the visibility, mentorship, and market access needed to bridge the gap between brilliant ideas and execution.

🏢

Corporate & CSR

Scale your impact through strategic partnership. We connect your CSR initiatives and ESG goals with measurable grassroots action, ensuring your investments drive high-impact, transparent, and sustainable outcomes.

🌾

Rural & Grassroots

The most effective solutions often come from those closest to the land. We amplify local voices, connecting traditional wisdom to national platforms and unlocking new economic opportunities for rural innovators.

🏛️

Government & Policy Makers

Aligning grassroots action with national vision. we provide a collaborative space to align regulatory frameworks with ground-level realities, ensuring national climate goals are met through community-driven success.
