Help Desk

You can edit the **Title**, **Author**, **Category**, **Description**, **Meta Title**, **Meta Description**, **Meta Keywords**, **Content**, and **Status** of the article.

Yes, both **Author** and **Category** must be selected to properly attribute and categorize the article.

The **Content** field is where you write the main body of your article. You can use the rich text editor provided to format your text, add images, and more.

These fields (**Meta Title**, **Meta Description**, and **Meta Keywords**) are crucial for SEO. They help search engines understand and index your article, improving its visibility in search results.

The **Status** field controls whether the article is live and visible to the public on your website. Setting it to "Enabled" will make it visible, while "Disabled" will hide it.

Typically, the **Slug** (the URL-friendly version of the title) is generated automatically when the article is created. Depending on your system, changing the Title *might* not automatically update the Slug. You might have a separate process to regenerate slugs if needed.
