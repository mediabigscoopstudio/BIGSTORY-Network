Help Desk

You can edit the **Title**, **Description**, **Meta Title**, **Meta Description**, **Meta Keywords**, and **Status** of the category.

Yes, the **Title** field is mandatory. You need to provide a title to identify the category.

These fields (**Meta Title**, **Meta Description**, and **Meta Keywords**) are important for Search Engine Optimization (SEO). They help search engines understand the content of pages associated with this category.

The **Status** field allows you to control whether the category is currently active and visible on your website. You can set it to "Enabled" or "Disabled".

Typically, the **Slug** (the URL-friendly version of the title) is generated automatically when the category is created. Depending on your system, changing the Title *might* not automatically update the Slug. You might have a separate process to regenerate slugs if needed.
